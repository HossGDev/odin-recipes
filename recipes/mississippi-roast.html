Mississippi Roast

Description

a traditional American Southern dish consisting of a piece of beef that is slow-cooked in a rich, savory sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef roast
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1/2 cup red wine
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup butter

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cut the beef roast into chunks.
  3. In a large pot, melt the butter and sauté the onions and garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the flour and stir for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the beef broth and red wine, and bring to a boil.
  6. Add the beef chunks to the pot and simmer for 2 hours.
