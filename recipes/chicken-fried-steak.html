Chicken Fried Steak

Description

a traditional American Southern dish consisting of a piece of beefsteak that is coated in seasoned flour, dipped in batter, and fried using the exact same technique as Southern fried chicken

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beefsteak
  • 1 cup seasoned flour
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Preheat oil to 350°F.
  2. Coat the beefsteak in seasoned flour.
  3. Dip the coated beefsteak in a mixture of milk and egg.
  4. Roll the beefsteak in breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry the beefsteak in the preheated oil until golden brown.
