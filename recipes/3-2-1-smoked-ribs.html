3-2-1 Smoked Ribs

Description

A delicious and easy-to-make smoked ribs recipe that uses a 3-2-1 method for perfect results.

Ingredients

  • 1 rack of pork ribs
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup paprika
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the smoker to 225°F.
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne pepper, and black pepper.
  3. Apply the spice mixture to the ribs and let them rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Place the ribs on the smoker and cook for 3 hours.
  5. Wrap the ribs in foil and cook for 2 more hours.
  6. Unwrap the ribs and cook for 1 more hour.
  7. Serve with barbecue sauce.
